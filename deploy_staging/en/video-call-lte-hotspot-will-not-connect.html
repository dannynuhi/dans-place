How to fix video call when your LTE hotspot will not connect

If video call on your LTE hotspot will not connect, start with the simple checks below. These steps are calm, reversible, and meant to help you narrow the cause before trying anything advanced.

Start here

  1. Confirm the LTE hotspot has power, is awake, and is close enough to any needed accessory or network.
  2. Close the app or settings screen related to video call, then open it again and try the same action once.
  3. Turn the affected connection or feature off for ten seconds, then turn it back on.
  4. Restart the LTE hotspot. A clean restart often clears stuck background tasks without changing your files.
  5. Check for a pending system or app update if video call still will not connect. Install only updates from trusted settings or app stores.
  6. Try one known-good comparison, such as another cable, another network, another account, or another device.
  7. If the problem continues, write down the exact error text and any recent change before asking for support.

What to check next

Move slowly and test after each step. If one change helps, stop there and keep the setup simple.

Safety note

Avoid deleting accounts, resetting the whole device, or changing advanced network settings until you have a backup and a clear reason to do so.
